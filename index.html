What we did

Make your own game

Want to make one too? It takes three steps. Younger students should do this with a grown-up.

  1. Chat with the AI helper

    Open our school AI space and tell it about your game: what subject, what it looks like, what happens when you win. It will help you build a game plan.

    Open the school AI helper ↗

    Join code: 5K67-WF3L

  2. Copy your prompt

    When the helper gives you your finished prompt, select all of it and copy it (Ctrl+C, or ⌘+C on a Mac).

  3. Paste it into Gemini and play

    Go to gemini.google.com with a parent or guardian, paste the prompt into the chat box and send it. If it asks, say you want a single HTML file game.

    Try your game in the preview. If something is wrong, tell Gemini what to change ("make the buttons bigger", "add a score") and keep going until you love it. This is just for you to enjoy at home!

Use AI the 4D way

At DHA we use AI to think further, not to think less. Keep the 4 D's in mind whenever you make something with AI. Tap the poster to see it bigger.

Tips for great games

  • Keep it focused: one subject and one clear goal.
  • Ask for big buttons, bright colours and a score.
  • Test it a few times, then ask Gemini to fix anything odd (that is the Double-Check D).
  • Never share personal details (full name, school email, address) in a game or chat.
